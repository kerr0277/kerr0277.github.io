The Excellence of BMW

BMW is recognized worldwide for combining luxury, performance, and precision engineering. Every vehicle is carefully designed to deliver a dynamic driving experience while maintaining exceptional comfort and safety.

From innovative engine technology to advanced safety systems, BMW continues to set high standards in the automotive industry. The brand represents quality, reliability, and driving excellence.

Facts About BMW

  • BMW was founded in 1916 in Germany.
  • The name BMW stands for Bayerische Motoren Werke.
  • BMW originally manufactured aircraft engines.
  • The BMW M division produces high-performance vehicles.
